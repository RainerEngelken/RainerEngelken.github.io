The Power of Superposition

Instead of solving for new integration constants at every switch event, we track the response components globally:

  • ZIR izi(t): The initial energy present at t=0 decaying through all future circuit topologies.
  • ZSR izs(t): The response to the input injected later, assuming initial energy was zero.
